Model Comparison Results

Standardized evaluation across three test datasets. All models use U-Net with ResNet34 encoder, trained on combined datasets.

Metric Definitions

Mean Dice / IoU: Calculated per image, then averaged. Sensitive to performance on individual images.
Global Dice / IoU: Aggregates pixel intersections and mask counts across dataset before computing. Weights larger objects more.

Key Findings

✅ RGB + Depth Wins Overall

  • • Highest Mean Dice on all three datasets
  • • Highest Global Dice on all three datasets
  • • Largest improvement on CAMO (+24.7% over RGB)

⚠️ Canny Edge Results Mixed

  • • Helps on CAMO (0.641 vs 0.597 Mean Dice)
  • • Slightly hurts on COD10K (0.624 vs 0.638)
  • • Negligible effect on NC4K (0.754 vs 0.755)

🔍 Combined Not Better Than Depth Alone

  • • RGB+Canny+Depth < RGB+Depth on all metrics
  • • Extra channel may add noise without benefit
  • • Depth provides sufficient geometric cues

📊 Global vs Mean Metrics

  • • Global metrics consistently higher
  • • Larger objects dominate global scores
  • • Mean metrics better reflect per-image quality
